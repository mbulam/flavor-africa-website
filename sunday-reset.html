Flavor Playlist

Sunday Reset

Flavor Playlist Vol. 01: six songs, from the slow start to golden hour.

Flavor Africa · 25 Oct 2026
  1. 01

    By Your Side

    Sade
    The slow start · 2000 · from Lovers Rock

    The lead single from Lovers Rock is the gentlest way into a Sunday: Ibadan-born Sade, promising to stay, over a groove that never hurries.

  2. 02

    Jailer

    Aṣa
    Windows open · 2007 · from Aṣa

    The opening track of her debut, produced by Cobhams Asuquo. It sounds soft, but Aṣa wrote it with Nigeria’s years of dictatorship in mind. Let go of whatever is holding you.

  3. 03

    People

    Libianca
    Check on yourself · 2022 · single

    The Cameroonian singer wrote it from her own mental health journey, and it went all the way to No. 2 in the UK. Take it as your cue to check in on yourself, and on your people.

  4. 04

    I Choose You

    Bridget Blue & Bien
    Soft afternoon · 2024 · single

    Bridget Blue teamed up with Sauti Sol’s Bien for a duet that keeps love simple. Two warm voices, one easy decision.

  5. 05

    Lucy

    Bensoul
    The pick-me-up · 2019
  6. 06

    Wish You Were Here

    Black Coffee feat. Msaki
    Golden hour · 2018

Save this for next Sunday. Which one is on repeat?
