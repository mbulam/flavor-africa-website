Lagos Fashion Week 2026

Everything to know before the runway lights up. 28 October to 1 November, the Crafted season.

Flavor Fashion · 28 Oct – 1 Nov 2026

This year’s themeHeritage. Innovation. Circular futures.

This year’s theme is CRAFTED. The season asks how African fashion can draw on its history while finding new ways to design and make clothes that last.

Since 2011Fifteen years in the making

Founded by Omoyemi Akerele, Lagos Fashion Week has grown into one of the continent’s most important stages for African design.

The incubatorWhere the next big names start

Its Fashion Focus Fund helped launch Orange Culture, Kenneth Ize, IAMISIGO, Emmy Kasbit and Ejiro Amos Tafiri.

28 Oct – 1 NovFive days of African design

We’ll be bringing you the standout looks all week. Which designer are you watching?
