About flavor.africa

Global culture,
African flavor.

Flavor Africa is an entertainment magazine based in Nairobi. We cover music, film and TV, fashion, sport and the business side of entertainment, in Kenya, across Africa and around the world.

What we do

We publish entertainment news every day, along with longer features, reviews, charts and a weekly guide to what's on in Nairobi. Our stories are written in full, not as one-line captions, and every fact is checked against at least two sources before it goes up.

Our coverage starts at home and goes outward. A Kenyan release, an album out of Lagos and a Hollywood premiere can all sit on the same page, and we look at each one with an African audience in mind.

Away from the page, we run Flavor Artist Live, our own live show series, and we host and interview artists.
