The Watchlist

Must watch this weekend

Haymitch gets his own Hunger Games, Anele Mdoda lets the cameras into her wedding, and Netflix has Cinderella's stepsisters, the FTX story and a Brazilian drama ready. Five picks for 20–22 November.

The Watchlist · 20–22 November 2026 · 5 min read

One big cinema release and four for the couch. The new Hunger Games film opens this weekend, South Africa's most talked-about wedding is on Netflix, and the streamer has three new titles worth your time. Here's what to watch, where, and why.

  1. 01

    The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping

    Joseph Zada, Whitney Peak
    In cinemas, including IMAX · from Friday 20 November

    Set 24 years before Katniss, the new Hunger Games film goes back to the 50th Games, the Second Quarter Quell. Joseph Zada plays a 16-year-old Haymitch Abernathy, the role Woody Harrelson played in the original films, with Whitney Peak as his girlfriend, Lenore Dove Baird.

    Francis Lawrence directs again, and Jesse Plemons is in the cast. Critics are split on it: TheWrap calls it one of the best films in the series, while the South China Morning Post found it by the numbers. It was filmed for IMAX, and in Nairobi, Century Cinemax at Garden City Mall has an IMAX screen.

  2. 02

    Stars and Vows: Anele & Buzza

    Anele Mdoda, Bonelela “Buzza James” Mgudlwa
    Netflix · out since Friday 13 November

    Anele Mdoda and her husband, Bonelela “Buzza James” Mgudlwa, have known each other since their teens, and this four-part special lets the cameras in on the wedding, the families and the traditions around it.

    Nomzamo Mbatha is one of the producers, and the show has been all over South African timelines since it landed. It's a week old, but if you missed it, this is the weekend.

  3. 03

    Steps

    Ali Wong, Stephanie Hsu, Amanda Seyfried
    Netflix · from Friday 20 November

    Netflix's new animated film tells Cinderella from the other side of the family. Ali Wong voices Lilith, a stepsister so tired of living in Cinderella's shadow that she steals the Fairy Godmother's wand and hijacks the Royal Ball with her sister Margot, voiced by Stephanie Hsu.

    Amanda Seyfried is Cinderella and Bette Midler is the Fairy Godmother, with Nikki Glaser, Daniel Radcliffe, Peter Dinklage and Young Mazino also in the cast. Alyce Tzue and John Ripa, co-director of Raya and the Last Dragon, direct. It's the first film made fully in-house at Netflix Animation Studios.

  4. 04

    The Altruists

    Julia Garner, Anthony Boyle
    Netflix · all eight episodes from Thursday 19 November

    Before FTX collapsed in 2022, Sam Bankman-Fried and Caroline Ellison were two young idealists who thought they could remake the financial system. This limited series follows how that ended, with Anthony Boyle as Bankman-Fried and Julia Garner as Ellison.

    Graham Moore and Jacqueline Hoyt run the show, and Alex Lawther, Matt Rife and Madison Hu are in the cast. It's inspired by New York magazine's reporting on the case.

  5. 05

    On Behalf of My Son

    Rejane Faria
    Netflix · from Friday 20 November

    Vicentina's son Wesley was driving the bus when it crashed, and he died in it. As the investigation suggests he may have done it on purpose, she sets out to find the victims' families and apologise to each of them in person.

    Rejane Faria leads this Brazilian drama, written and directed by Gabriel Martins, who made Mars One. It played in competition at San Sebastián and at Toronto before landing on Netflix. It's a slow, heavy watch, so save it for a quiet evening.
