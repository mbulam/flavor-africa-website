Grammy nominations 2027: the main categories and the African races

RAYE and Rosalía lead the field, Beyoncé reaches 100 career nominations, and Tems is up for four awards. Here are the nominees.

The Wrap · Global · 16 Nov 2026 · 4 min read

The Recording Academy announced the nominees for the 69th Grammy Awards on Monday, 16 November 2026. For the first time, all 100 categories were read out on the livestream, which started at 3:45pm Nairobi time. The winners will be named on Sunday, 7 February 2027, at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, with the show airing on ABC, Disney+ and Hulu.

RAYE and Rosalía lead this year’s nominations, Beyoncé became the first artist to reach 100 career nominations, and Tems has four. Below are the nominees in the four main categories and the categories where African artists are competing.

General fieldSong of the Year

  • BerghainRosalía, Björk & Yves Tumor
  • Choosin’ TexasElla Langley

Song of the Year is a songwriting award, so the trophy goes to the writers. The artist who recorded each song is listed here.

General fieldBest New Artist

  • Angine de Poitrine
  • Geese

  • Slayyyter
  • Sienna Spiro
  • Don Toliver
  • Remi Wolf

AfricaBest African Music Performance

  • GratitudeAsake
  • Aye Kan (Are You Coming Back?)Angélique Kidjo & Ayra Starr
  • No Tradition, No AfricaKing Ayisoba featuring Rocky Dawuni
  • Big DaddyTems
  • JogodoWizkid & Asake

Asake is in this category twice, once on his own with “Gratitude” and once with Wizkid on “Jogodo”. That song is Wizkid’s seventh career Grammy nomination.

Tyla won this award in 2024 with “Water” and again in 2026 with “Push 2 Start”, but she is not nominated this year. That leaves Tems, who won it in 2025 with “Love Me JeJe”, as the only past winner in the field.

GlobalBest Global Music Album

  • EquilibrivmAnitta
  • OriadéDavido
  • Hope!!Angélique Kidjo
  • StarrgirlAyra Starr
  • Love Is a KingdomTems

Four of the five albums in this category are by African artists. The only exception is Anitta’s Equilibrivm.

GlobalBest Global Music Performance

  • VaruvaaiyDevan Ekambaram featuring Susha
  • Una Flor En Cada EstrellaKerreKe, Mireya & Alex E. Chávez
  • Bando [Orchestral]Angélique Kidjo featuring Pharrell Williams & Quavo
  • PorcelanaRosalía & Dougie F
  • Dai DaiShakira & Burna Boy

Burna Boy is back in this category with Shakira on “Dai Dai”. For Angélique Kidjo, “Bando” is her third nomination this year, after “Aye Kan” and her album Hope!!.

R&amp;BBest R&amp;B Solo Performance

  • Morning Dew (Donk)Beyoncé
  • Out the WindowKehlani
  • Let MeVictoria Monét
  • PresshaJill Scott
  • What You NeedTems

Beyoncé’s nomination for “Morning Dew (Donk)” is the 100th of her career. No artist has reached that number before. Tems is also nominated for Best R&B Song for “What You Need”, which she wrote with GuiltyBeatz.

RapBest Rap Song

  • ErrtimeCardi B
